# Search and source-verification protocol

## Scope

The review targeted four linked literatures:

1. Kaldorian manufacturing, increasing returns, and cumulative causation;
2. balance-of-payments-constrained growth and capital-flow extensions;
3. Chilean industrialization, imported capital goods, and external crises;
4. Chilean deindustrialization and structural change after 1973.

## Inclusion rules

A source was retained when:

- authorship, year, title, and venue could be verified;
- a DOI, publisher record, official institutional page, or official repository was available;
- the source supplied evidence for a defined link in the mechanism;
- its geography, period, and inferential limit could be stated.

## Exclusion rules

The review excluded:

- unverified citations and incomplete bibliographic records;
- general summaries when the primary or official source was available;
- evidence that duplicated a stronger source without adding a distinct mechanism;
- panel results presented as Chile-specific causal evidence;
- historical narratives used to override a failed statistical gate.

## Search logic

Search strings combined terms for Kaldor, Verdoorn, cumulative causation, Thirlwall, balance-of-payments constraint, capital flows, Chile, manufacturing, imported capital goods, real exchange rate, deindustrialization, and the 1961 crisis. Backward and forward citation tracing linked the foundational theory to modern empirical and Chile-specific work.
