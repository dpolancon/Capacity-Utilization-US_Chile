## Evidence verdict

The literature establishes that Chile's productive transformation and external constraint are jointly meaningful objects. It does not establish that gross machinery capital is \(I(1)\), that the observed RER generates statistically distinct adjustment regimes, or that an import ECT alone identifies binding dates. Those are empirical gates for this pipeline, and their failure cannot be repaired by stronger narrative evidence.
