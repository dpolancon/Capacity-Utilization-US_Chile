## 1. Executive Summary & Overview

This document provides the definitive analytical, empirical, and architectural guideline for the harmonized Real GDP, Nominal GDP, and GDP Deflator datasets constructed for **Chile (1860–2025)** and the **United States (1929–2025)**. 

In long-run macroeconomic analysis, growth accounting, and cointegration modeling of capital accumulation, output must be deflated consistently without introducing arbitrary structural breaks or filter-induced distortions. This dataset establishes unified macro-aggregate and sector-boundary output series, providing the required nominal level, real output level ($Y$), and implicit price deflator ($P_Y$) series for both economies.

> [!NOTE]
> All quantitative datasets generated by this pipeline are stored in `data/processed/gdp_deflator/` for pipeline consumption and mirrored to `output/gdp_deflator/csv/` for reporting and analytical presentation.

---

## 2. Sector Boundaries & Analytical Scope

The dataset incorporates two explicit institutional and accounting boundaries:

1. **Aggregate Macro Economy (Total GDP)**:
   - **Chile**: Total Gross Domestic Product ($Y_{\text{CL}}$), covering 1860–2025. Measured in millions of 2003 Chilean Pesos (CLP) for real output and indexed to $2003 = 100$ for the price deflator.
   - **United States**: Total Gross Domestic Product ($Y_{\text{US, Macro}}$), covering 1929–2025. Measured in millions of chained 2017 US Dollars (USD) for real output and indexed to $2017 = 100$ for the implicit price deflator.

2. **Non-Financial Corporate Sector (NFC GVA)**:
   - **United States**: Non-Financial Corporate Gross Value Added ($Y_{\text{US, NFC}}$), covering 1929–2024. Measured in millions of 2017-equivalent USD for real value added and indexed to $2017 = 100$ for the same-boundary implicit NFC price deflator derived from BEA NIPA Table 1.14.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Boundary Discipline**: Macroeconomic aggregate GDP deflators ($P_{Y,\text{GDP}}$) and Non-Financial Corporate GVA deflators ($P_{Y,\text{NFC}}$) are maintained as distinct objects. Per repo governance, total aggregate deflators must never be silently substituted for sector-specific corporate deflators.

## 3. Empirical Data Provenance & Splicing Methodology

### 3.1. Chile Real GDP and Deflator (1860–2025)

Chile’s long-run historical accounts combine benchmark historical research with live official national accounts:

- **1860–2010 Benchmark Series**: Historical real GDP is anchored on Pérez & Eyzaguirre's aggregate demand reconstruction (*Demanda Agregada de Chile 1860-2010*), expressed in millions of constant 2003 CLP. Historical GDP deflators are drawn from ClioLab PUC (Table 4.1.3, series `DEFPGB`), anchored to $2003 = 100$.
- **2010–2025 Live Splice**: Modern real GDP and price deflators are retrieved directly from the Central Bank of Chile (Banco Central de Chile - BCCh) API Siete:
  - Real GDP flow series: `F032.PIB.FLU.R.CLP.EP18.Z.Z.0.T` (quarterly flow aggregated to annual sum).
  - Annual GDP Deflator series: `F032.PIB.DEF.N.CLP.EP18.Z.Z.0.A`.
- **Splicing Formula**: Post-2010 extension applies multiplicative growth-rate chaining to preserve historical levels while incorporating official modern growth dynamics:
  $$Y_{t, \text{real}}^{\text{spliced}} = Y_{t-1, \text{real}}^{\text{spliced}} \times \left( \frac{\text{BCCh}_t}{\text{BCCh}_{t-1}} \right), \quad t > 2010$$
  $$P_{Y, t}^{\text{spliced}} = P_{Y, t-1}^{\text{spliced}} \times \left( \frac{\text{BCCh\_DEF}_t}{\text{BCCh\_DEF}_{t-1}} \right), \quad t > 2010$$
- **Nominal GDP Derivation**:
  $$\text{GDP}_{t, \text{nominal}}^{\text{CLP}} = \text{GDP}_{t, \text{real}}^{\text{CLP, 2003}} \times \left( \frac{P_{Y, t}}{100} \right)$$

### 3.2. United States Real GDP and Deflator (1929–2025)

The United States series relies directly on the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA) National Income and Product Accounts (NIPA):

- **Aggregate Nominal GDP**: NIPA Table 1.1.5 Line 1 (retrieved via FRED series `GDPA`), converted from billions of current dollars to millions of current dollars ($\text{GDPA} \times 1000$).
- **Implicit GDP Deflator**: NIPA Table 1.1.9 Line 1 (retrieved via FRED series `A191RD3A086NBEA`), index $2017 = 100$.
- **Aggregate Real GDP Derivation**:
  $$\text{GDP}_{t, \text{real}}^{\text{USD, 2017}} = \frac{\text{GDP}_{t, \text{nominal}}^{\text{USD, mil}}}{P_{Y, t, \text{GDP}} / 100}$$
- **NFC GVA & Output Price Layer (S12B Protocol)**:
  - Nominal NFC GVA: NIPA Table 1.14 Line 17 (`A455RC`, millions of current dollars).
  - Real NFC GVA Baseline: NIPA Table 1.14 Line 41 (`B455RX`, millions of chained 2017 dollars).
  - NFC Implicit Deflator ($P_{Y, \text{NFC}}$): $100 \times \frac{\text{Line 17}}{\text{Line 41}}$.

## 4. Master Variable Dictionary & Data Schema

The processed CSV files adopt a clean, self-describing schema:

| Column Header | Data Type | Units / Basis | Description & Analytical Definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| `country` | String | Country Name | Full country identifier (`Chile` or `United States`). |
| `iso3` | String | ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 | 3-letter country code (`CHL` or `USA`). |
| `year` | Integer | Annual (YYYY) | Observation year (1860–2025 for Chile, 1929–2025 for US). |
| `sector_boundary` | String | Institutional Boundary | Scope of output (`Total Economy (Aggregate GDP)` or `Non-Financial Corporate (NFC GVA)`). |
| `local_currency` | String | ISO Currency Code | `CLP` for Chile, `USD` for United States. |
| `gdp_nominal_mil` | Float | Local Currency (Mil) | Nominal Output in current prices ($Y_{\text{nominal}}$). |
| `gdp_real_mil` | Float | Constant Price (Mil) | Real Output level ($Y_{\text{real}}$), benchmarked to base year. |
| `gdp_deflator_index` | Float | Index Ratio ($\times 100$) | Implicit Price Deflator ($P_Y$), normalized to Base Year $= 100$. |
| `base_year` | Integer | Benchmark Year | Reference base year ($2003$ for CLP, $2017$ for USD). |
| `source_provenance` | String | Provenance Track | Audit trail listing source tables, API codes, and splice rules. |
