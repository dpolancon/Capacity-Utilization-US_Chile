# U.S. Distributive Variable and Profit-Share Measurement Ledger

## 1. Decision

This ledger identifies the U.S. distributive variables already constructed in
the repository, the additional measures that can be constructed from available
income-account ingredients, and the objects that remain blocked or rejected.
It applies the Shaikh-Tonak distinction between productive-origin surplus and
the subsequent distribution, transfer, and absorption of that surplus.

The verdict is fixed:

- `NFC_COMPENSATION_SHARE_GVA` remains the authorized distributive baseline.
- Profit-share measures remain diagnostics, sensitivity measures, or proposed
  constructions unless a later stage contract explicitly authorizes their use.
- `NFC_NOS` remains the locked productive-origin surplus level.
- No object in this ledger bypasses the integration-order gate or authorizes a
  new regressor.
- Chile is outside this ledger and requires a separate measurement audit.

The spelling **Tonak** is used because it is the spelling adopted by the
repository's Shaikh-Tonak accounting doctrine.

## 2. Governing accounting boundary

The financial corporate sector is treated as nonproductive in the
surplus-value sense. Its income is a claim on, redistribution of, or absorption
of value generated in productive activity. The observable productive-origin
corporate surplus proxy is therefore:

$$
S_t^{origin} = NFC\_NOS_t
$$

The implied financial account is an accounting residual:

$$
FIN\_X\_IMPLIED_t = CORP\_X_t - NFC\_X_t
$$

For net operating surplus:

$$
FIN\_NOS\_IMPLIED_t = CORP\_NOS_t - NFC\_NOS_t
$$

This residual is not an independently observed flow and is not an additional
surplus component that can be added back to `CORP_NOS`. Because `CORP_NOS`
already contains both the NFC and implied-financial components, adding
`FIN_NOS_IMPLIED` to it would count the financial component twice.

## 7. Proposed profit-share hierarchy

The hierarchy ranks interpretations. It does not promote a new regressor.

### Rank 1 - net productive-origin surplus share

Use the existing series:

$$
\pi_{NFC,NVA,t}^{NOS} = \frac{NFC\_NOS_t}{NFC\_NVA_t}
$$

This is the cleanest available share for a net productive-origin surplus
concept because the numerator and denominator use the NFC boundary and both
exclude CFC. It remains `CONSTRUCTED_DIAGNOSTIC`.

### Rank 2 - productive-origin NOS with a gross denominator

Use the existing series:

$$
\pi_{NFC,GVA,t}^{NOS} = \frac{NFC\_NOS_t}{NFC\_GVA_t}
$$

This measure preserves the current S29A/D10 interface and is useful for
comparison with the GVA wage-share baseline. Because it combines net NOS with
gross value added, it is a gross-denominator robustness measure rather than a
fully net distributive partition.

### Rank 3 - gross operating-surplus share

The ingredients permit:

$$
NFC\_GOS_t = NFC\_NOS_t + NFC\_CFC_t
$$

$$
\pi_{NFC,GVA,t}^{GOS} =
\frac{NFC\_NOS_t + NFC\_CFC_t}{NFC\_GVA_t}
$$

| Proposed variable ID | Status | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| `NFC_GROSS_OPERATING_SURPLUS_DERIVED` | `AVAILABLE_CONSTRUCTIBLE` | Derived gross operating surplus; add CFC once to NOS. |
| `NFC_GROSS_OPERATING_SURPLUS_SHARE_GVA_DERIVED` | `AVAILABLE_CONSTRUCTIBLE` | Gross productive-origin operating-surplus share of gross value added. |

This gross measure is accounting-consistent, but it must not be described as
net profit and CFC must not be added again downstream.

### Rank 4 - after-financial-claims sensitivity

Use the existing S30G shares:

$$
\pi_{NFC,NVA,t}^{after\ claims} =
\frac{NFC\_NOS_t - NFC\_NET\_INT_t}{NFC\_NVA_t}
$$

$$
\pi_{NFC,GVA,t}^{after\ claims} =
\frac{NFC\_NOS_t - NFC\_NET\_INT_t}{NFC\_GVA_t}
$$

These measure remaining surplus after one bounded net-interest and
miscellaneous-payments line. They do not replace the productive-origin
measure.

### Rank 5 - profit appropriation shares

| Proposed variable ID | Formula | Account meaning | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `NFC_PROFITS_IVA_CC_SHARE_NVA` | `NFC_PROFITS_IVA_CC / NFC_NVA` | NFC corporate profits with IVA and CCAdj relative to NVA. | `AVAILABLE_CONSTRUCTIBLE` |
| `NFC_PROFITS_IVA_CC_SHARE_GVA` | `NFC_PROFITS_IVA_CC / NFC_GVA` | Same adjusted-profit concept with a gross denominator. | `AVAILABLE_CONSTRUCTIBLE` |
| `NFC_AFTER_TAX_SHARE_NVA` | `NFC_AFTER_TAX / NFC_NVA` | Profits after tax with IVA and CCAdj relative to NVA. | `AVAILABLE_CONSTRUCTIBLE` |
| `NFC_PBT_SHARE_NVA` | `NFC_PBT / NFC_NVA` | Profits before tax without IVA and CCAdj relative to NVA. | `AVAILABLE_CONSTRUCTIBLE` |
| `NFC_PAT_SHARE_NVA` | `NFC_PAT / NFC_NVA` | Profits after tax without IVA and CCAdj relative to NVA. | `AVAILABLE_CONSTRUCTIBLE` |
| `NFC_UNDISTRIBUTED_SHARE_NVA` | `NFC_UNDISTRIBUTED / NFC_NVA` | Undistributed profits with IVA and CCAdj; a retention/appropriation share. | `AVAILABLE_CONSTRUCTIBLE` |
| `NFC_RETAINED_SHARE_NVA` | `NFC_RETAINED / NFC_NVA` | Undistributed after-tax profits without IVA and CCAdj; a retention share. | `AVAILABLE_CONSTRUCTIBLE` |

These measures answer different questions from NOS. Interest and transfers
bridge NOS to profits; taxes bridge profits to after-tax profits; dividends
bridge after-tax profits to undistributed profits. They must remain separately
named and cannot be stacked as additional components of surplus.

### Rank 6 - corporate-boundary comparisons

Corporate analogues are constructible only where S24A contains the matching
corporate numerator and denominator. This includes NOS, derived GOS, profits
with IVA and CCAdj, before-tax profits, after-tax profits, and undistributed
profits. S24A does not contain a `CORP_RETAINED` counterpart to `NFC_RETAINED`,
so that specific analogue is unavailable. Every available corporate measure
remains a broad comparison because the corporate boundary includes financial
corporations. None can replace the NFC productive-origin measures without a
theory-lock revision.

## 8. Residual complements are not NOS shares

The residual complement to the wage share is:

$$
r_{NFC,GVA,t}^{noncomp} =
1 - \frac{NFC\_COMP_t}{NFC\_GVA_t}
$$

| Proposed variable ID | Formula | Status and verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `NFC_NONCOMPENSATION_RESIDUAL_SHARE_GVA` | `1 - NFC_COMPENSATION_SHARE_GVA` | `AVAILABLE_CONSTRUCTIBLE`; residual non-compensation share only. |
| `NFC_NONCOMPENSATION_RESIDUAL_SHARE_NVA` | `1 - NFC_COMPENSATION_SHARE_NVA` | `AVAILABLE_CONSTRUCTIBLE`; residual non-compensation share only. |
| `CORP_NONCOMPENSATION_RESIDUAL_SHARE_GVA` | `1 - CORP_COMPENSATION_SHARE_GVA` | `AVAILABLE_CONSTRUCTIBLE`; broad corporate residual only. |
| `CORP_NONCOMPENSATION_RESIDUAL_SHARE_NVA` | `1 - CORP_COMPENSATION_SHARE_NVA` | `AVAILABLE_CONSTRUCTIBLE`; broad corporate residual only. |

The residual contains every non-compensation component admitted by the
denominator's accounting identity. It is not automatically equal to NOS/GVA or
NOS/NVA. Taxes on production and other account components prevent the silent
identity `profit share = 1 - wage share`.

The older `Psh_NF = 1 - Wsh_NF` series in `RAW_LEGACY` is therefore preserved
only as a legacy residual-share diagnostic. It must not be relabeled as NOS or
an exact profit share.

## 9. Exploitation-rate alternatives

Exploitation-rate variables are surplus-to-compensation ratios, not shares of
value added:

| Proposed variable ID | Formula | Interpretation | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `NFC_NOS_TO_COMPENSATION_RATIO` | `NFC_NOS / NFC_COMP` | Productive-origin NOS relative to compensation. | `AVAILABLE_CONSTRUCTIBLE` |
| `NFC_AFTER_INTEREST_SURPLUS_TO_COMPENSATION_RATIO` | `(NFC_NOS - NFC_NET_INT) / NFC_COMP` | Remaining surplus after bounded claims relative to compensation. | `AVAILABLE_CONSTRUCTIBLE` |
| `CORP_NOS_TO_COMPENSATION_RATIO` | `CORP_NOS / CORP_COMP` | Broad corporate NOS relative to corporate compensation. | `AVAILABLE_CONSTRUCTIBLE`; comparison only. |

The legacy `e_NF = Psh_NF / Wsh_NF` is a ratio of two complementary residual
shares. It is not identical to `NFC_NOS / NFC_COMP` and cannot substitute for
the proposed account-based ratio without an explicit definition change.

## 11. Double-accounting safeguards

| Risk | Required safeguard | Prohibited operation |
|---|---|---|
| Sector nesting | Choose NFC or corporate totals for a measure. Treat `CORP - NFC` as an implied-financial residual. | `CORP + NFC`, `CORP + FIN_IMPLIED`, or `NFC + FIN_IMPLIED + CORP`. |
| Productive origin versus redistribution | Use `NFC_NOS` for productive-origin surplus. Use after-interest measures only for remaining-surplus sensitivity. | Replacing `NFC_NOS` with an after-interest or financial-residual measure. |

## 12. Verified account identities

The S24A observations reconcile for every year from 1929 through 2025 within a
maximum absolute residual of one million current dollars, the precision of the
published integer-valued source rows.

Gross-to-net bridge:

$$
NVA_t = GVA_t - CFC_t
$$

NOS-to-profit bridge:

$$
NOS_t =
NET\_INT_t +
TRANSFERS\_NET_t +
PROFITS\_IVA\_CC_t
$$

Profit-tax bridge:

$$
PROFITS\_IVA\_CC_t = AFTER\_TAX_t + TAX_t
$$

Distribution bridge:

$$
AFTER\_TAX_t =
DIVIDENDS\_NET_t +
UNDISTRIBUTED_t
$$

These identities pass for both NFC and corporate accounts. The rounding
tolerance does not authorize combining adjusted and unadjusted profit concepts.
