When Does Chile’s Balance-of-Payments Constraint
Bind?
A reproducible RER-controlled import-system audit, 1920–2024
Chapter 2 research pipeline
29 July 2026

Abstract
This report rebuilds the Chilean import-system analysis from source snapshots through
a frozen release, exact split samples, formal integration-order gates, RER-controlled VECMs,
observed-threshold conditional VECMs, and a triangulated balance-of-payments screening index.
The legacy four-variable long-run coefficients reproduce to a maximum absolute difference of
1.03e-10. The reproduction succeeds, but the promoted machinery model does not: log gross
machinery capital remains unresolved or at risk of I(2) in both 1920–1973 and 1974–2024. The
machinery VECM and machinery-defined threshold VECM are therefore blocked. Reduced
admissible systems find no RER-threshold effect (bootstrap p = 0.330 before 1974 and p = 0.573
after 1973). The resulting episode chronology is a screening device, not a validated latent-state
estimator.
Keywords: Chile; balance of payments; imports; machinery; real exchange rate; VECM;
threshold VECM; Kaldor; Thirlwall

Resumen
Este informe reconstruye el análisis chileno de importaciones desde las fuentes hasta una versión
de datos congelada. La reproducción de los coeficientes heredados es exacta, pero el capital bruto
de maquinaria no supera la compuerta de orden de integración en ninguna submuestra. Por tanto,
el VECM de maquinaria y su versión umbral no se promueven. Los sistemas reducidos tampoco
rechazan linealidad (p = 0.330 y p = 0.573). La cronología final triangula presión del ECT e
indicadores observados y debe leerse como diagnóstico de episodios, no como fechamiento definitivo.

Introduction and verdict

Chile’s industrialization made foreign exchange a condition of productive transformation: machinery
and intermediate imports expanded the productive apparatus, while weak export performance
periodically restricted the capacity to finance those imports. The mechanism makes an import
disequilibrium measure worth testing, but it does not authorize classifying crises from a residual
alone (Banco Central de Chile, 2025; Álvarez and Fuentes, 2003).

This paper examines whether the import requirement associated with non-reinvested surplus, gross
machinery accumulation, distribution, and the real exchange rate yields an admissible long-run
system and whether its adjustment identifies periods in which Chile’s balance-of-payments constraint
became binding. The answer is disciplined by the gate: the legacy relation is computationally
reproducible, but gross machinery capital fails the required I(1) classification in both politicaleconomic samples, so the central machinery VECM cannot carry the proposed inference.
The result separates replication from validation. Exact numerical reconciliation establishes that
the archived calculation has been recovered; it cannot establish that the level variables form an
admissible cointegrating system. The report consequently advances the reproducible data release
and reduced-system diagnostics while holding the requested machinery coefficients and machinery
ECT outside the paper-facing evidence.

Mechanism and identification

Kaldor’s mechanism runs from manufacturing expansion to increasing returns and productivity
growth; cumulative causation then makes productive structure path dependent (Kaldor, 1967, 1970).
Modern cross-country evidence supports the general manufacturing-growth mechanism but cannot
identify a Chilean coefficient (Marconi et al., 2016). Thirlwall’s closure makes sustained growth
conditional on export earnings and the income elasticity of imports, while later extensions admit
capital flows and debt service (Thirlwall, 1979; Thirlwall and Hussain, 1982; Moreno-Brid, 2003).
The combined theory predicts a foreign-exchange constraint on transformation, not a particular
time-series rank or crisis classifier.
For Chile, the connecting mechanism is imported productive inputs. Official history records that
CORFO-led industrialization raised capital- and intermediate-goods imports from the late 1940s,
while export stagnation left insufficient foreign exchange and produced the 1961 balance-of-payments
crisis (Banco Central de Chile, 2025). Plant-level analysis also emphasizes technology embodied in
imported capital and intermediate goods and their limited domestic substitutability (Álvarez and
Fuentes, 2003). These findings make the import–machinery relation substantively grounded; they
do not permit its stochastic properties to be assumed.
The post-1974 split is also substantive. Trade reform changed manufacturing composition and
productivity, import penetration weakened domestic interindustry linkages, and Chile’s development remained tied to copper and externally exposed structural change (Ffrench-Davis, 1983a,b,
2002; Albala-Bertrand, 2006; Palma, 2019; Solimano and Zapata-Román, 2024). A direct Chilean
application of balance-of-payments-constrained growth covers 1980–2019 but cannot adjudicate the
earlier system or the 1961 benchmark (Yunga Medina et al., 2022). The 1920–1973 and 1974–2024
estimates therefore represent distinct political-economic systems rather than an arbitrary mechanical
partition.

Conditional threshold test

The requested import–machinery threshold VECM is blocked by the same integration gate. The
reported nonlinear exercise is a reduced conditional system: imports, non-reinvested surplus, and
RER form the pre-1974 state with an I(0) wage-share control; imports, non-reinvested surplus,
and wage share form the post-1974 state with stationary ∆ log RERt as a control. The observed
threshold variable is annual ∆ log RERt , and the import ECT does not define its own regime.
The 15% trimmed search evaluates 160 candidates, imposes minimum regime sizes, and uses 999
conditional residual bootstrap replications in each sample. Table 3 shows that neither sample rejects
a linear adjustment process. The threshold estimates therefore locate descriptive profile minima;
they do not identify statistically distinct crisis regimes.

Robustness and specification space

The robustness stage covers alternative RER splice dates, ClioLab-only and spliced RER inputs,
deterministic terms, lag orders, adjacent sample boundaries, the 999-replication threshold bootstrap,
leave-one-episode-out checks, condition-number reporting, and confidence-band overlap. These
exercises expose sensitivity without using decimal equality as a gate. The machinery model remains
blocked throughout; stability of a held coefficient cannot substitute for integration admissibility.
The reduced Johansen ranks retain asymptotic trace evidence as a descriptive input because each
split exceeds 30 annual observations. A machinery-rank bootstrap is not reported because the
machinery estimator is blocked before rank promotion. This omission is a governed result rather than
missing computation: bootstrap refinement cannot rescue a state vector rejected by the upstream
gate.

8

Conclusion

The rebuild succeeds as an audit and rejects the central econometric shortcut. Chile’s importedmachinery and foreign-exchange mechanism is historically coherent, but the current gross machinery
series does not support the proposed split-sample machinery VECM. The reduced systems also
provide no formal evidence of RER-defined threshold adjustment. The report therefore retains the
BOP index as a transparent screening panel and directs the next research step toward resolving the
machinery stock’s stochastic properties and validating crisis dates against independent historical
records.

The empirical verdict changes the report’s contribution. It no longer claims to have estimated when
the external constraint became binding from a machinery ECT; it demonstrates which parts of that
claim reproduce, which parts pass stochastic gates, and where the evidence fails. That distinction
preserves the Kaldorian question while preventing a generated latent state from validating itself.

Data availability and reproducibility
The frozen modelling release is stored at data/releases/chile_bop_binding_v1/. Every report
table and figure is copied from completed stage ledgers. The report builder performs no estimation
and records the SHA-256 hash of each consumed input.

Limitations
The annual sample is small, the RER splice joins related but non-identical source definitions, the
gross machinery series fails the I(1) gate, the reduced Johansen ranks are descriptive rather than
bootstrapped rank claims, and the observed BOP indicators are incomplete proxies for reserve
scarcity, rationing, and access to finance. These limits bind the interpretation rather than appearing
as generic qualifications.
