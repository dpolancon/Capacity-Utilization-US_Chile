\section{Appendix: Order of Integration and Stochastic Pre-Testing Battery for the United States and Chile}
\label{app:integration_order}

\paragraph{A}
In Cointegrating Multivariate Polynomial Regressions (CMPR) estimated via Integrated Modified OLS (IM-OLS) \citep{VogelsangWagner2014,VogelsangWagner2024}, valid asymptotic inference and pivotal fixed-$b$ Wald distributions require all stochastic regressors to be integrated of order one ($I(1)$). When non-linear interaction terms ($k_t \times \omega_{t-1}$, $k_t \times \omega_{t-1} \times (1-\iota_{t-1})$) or regime-dependent threshold regressors ($k_t \times \omega_{t-1} \times \mathbf{1}\{Z_t \le \hat{\gamma}\}$) enter the long-run relation, establishing their stochastic integration order prior to estimation is an essential prerequisite. This appendix reports the complete unit-root and stationarity test batteries for both economies across all baseline series, non-linear interactions, and demand controls.

\paragraph{B}
Table~\ref{tab:sample_audit} outlines the sample boundaries, degree-of-freedom spans, and zero-NA estimation windows across the empirical dataset. For the United States, the stochastic pre-testing battery spans 1929--2024 ($N_{\text{lev}} = 96, N_{\text{diff}} = 95$). For Chile, the estimation window spans 1943--2010 ($N_{\text{lev}} = 68, N_{\text{diff}} = 67$).

\subsection{United States: Unit Root Test Batteries and Interaction Properties}

\paragraph{A}
We evaluate the stochastic properties of U.S. level variables and their interactions using five standard tests: Augmented Dickey-Fuller (ADF), DF-GLS (ERS), Phillips-Perron (PP), Kwiatkowski-Phillips-Schmidt-Shin (KPSS), and Zivot-Andrews (ZA) tests with endogenous structural breaks. For level specifications, test regressions include a deterministic trend. For first-differenced specifications, regressions specify an intercept. In the Zivot-Andrews procedure, structural breaks are estimated endogenously in both intercept and trend (\texttt{model = "both"}).

\paragraph{C}
Table~\ref{tab:urca_results_kcap} documents the integration properties of private core gross capital ($k^{\text{cap}}$) interacted with distributive wage shares and corporate IPP investment shares. Both Machinery and Equipment ($k^{\text{ME}}_t$) and Nonresidential Structures ($k^{\text{NRC}}_t$) are established $I(1)$ series. The apparent persistence in first-differenced capital composition ($\Delta \kappa_t$) under linear KPSS tests reflects the sharp structural deceleration of mechanization after 1973 (captured by the 1974 Zivot-Andrews break) rather than an $I(2)$ process (Perron, 1989). When capital is interacted with bounded wage shares and IPP shares ($k^{\text{cap}} \times \omega_i$ and $k^{\text{cap}} \times \omega_i \times \iota_t$), all double and triple interaction terms are confirmed to be strictly $I(1)$ ($ADF_{\text{diff}} < -6.89^{***}, KPSS_{\text{diff}} < 0.170$), ensuring valid CMPR estimation.
