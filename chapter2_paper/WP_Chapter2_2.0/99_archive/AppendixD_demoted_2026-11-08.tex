\section{Appendix D: Specification A - Full Estimation Results}
\label{app:specA_full_results}

This appendix reports the full estimation results for all 32
specifications of the aggregate capital model (Specification A).
The specifications span two interaction sets (Set 1: standard wage
interaction; Set 2: effective wage interaction scaled by
$(1-\iota_{t-1})$), two value-added bases (GVA and NVA), four wage
share measures per basis (NFC baseline, Corporate, Shaikh-Tonak
Tier 1, Shaikh-Tonak Tier 2), and two structural break modes (no
break dummies; Bai-Perron step dummies).
